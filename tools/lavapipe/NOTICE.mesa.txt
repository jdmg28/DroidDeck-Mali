Mesa vulkan driver package - lavapipe (llvmpipe behind Vulkan)

libvulkan_lvp.so and the libLLVM it is built against in this archive are taken unmodified from
Debian's own arm64 builds of the mesa-vulkan-drivers and libllvm19 source packages (see the version
in meta.json and the run summary of the workflow that produced this zip). Only the ELF dynamic section
of libvulkan_lvp.so is touched: DT_RUNPATH is set to $ORIGIN so that the loader finds the libLLVM
beside it after DroidDeck's importer renames the driver and places it in its own directory.
